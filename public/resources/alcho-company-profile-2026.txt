ABOUT
Since 2008, PT. NEW ALFA OMEGA UTAMA (Alcho Foods) has been a leading
Indonesian manufacturer of industrial-scale seasoning systems and premium
flavor solutions for the HORECA (Hotel, Restaurant & Catering) and OEM
food-manufacturing markets.

We deliver consistency, quality, and heritage in every gram — engineering
standardized flavor bases that let our partners scale without taste variance.

WHAT WE DO
- Sauces & Bumbu Dasar (foundational sauces and cooking bases)
- Bouillon & Seasoning (chicken/beef powders, broth bases, spices)
- Snack Seasoning (high-stability dry seasonings for FMCG snacks)
- OEM / Private Label manufacturing and proprietary flavor development

QUALITY & COMPLIANCE
- HALAL MUI certified product range
- FSSC 22000 food-safety management
- BPOM registered
- Laboratory-controlled blending for zero-variance batches
